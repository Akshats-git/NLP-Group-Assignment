Q4 Speed Demon benchmark
==============================================
Batch                       : 1000 corrupted words
Grammar trigger interval    : every 10 words (100 windows)

Segmentation + spelling     : 0.807 s total, 0.807 ms per word
Grammar trigger only        : 0.002 s total, 0.022 ms per window, 0.002 ms per word

Added by the per-token layer: 0.805 ms per word (369.2x the grammar layer)

Control batch of ordinary vocabulary words
Segmentation + spelling     : 0.386 ms per word
